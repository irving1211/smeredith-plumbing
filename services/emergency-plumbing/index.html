Plumbing and heating emergencies don't wait for business hours. Burst pipes, no heat in January, no hot water Saturday morning, gas leaks, sewage backups — Shane Meredith comes out personally, with parts on the truck, ready to make the call to fix and the call to wait until Monday.

What we handle

No heat in January

Boiler down, pilot out, circulator failed, no-fire. Same-day Saturday is the rule, not the exception. Most repairs done on the first visit.

No hot water

Tank failure, pilot out, thermocouple, control board. We diagnose first, swap the part if it's fixable, replace the unit same-day if it's not.

Burst or frozen pipe

We shut it down, thaw what's salvageable, repair or replace what isn't. Insurance-ready photos and invoicing every time.

Sewage or sump-pump failure

Backed-up drains, failed sump pumps, ejector pumps. Containment first, repair second, sanitation last.

Gas smell or suspected leak

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the utility first — then call us for the repair side. Licensed gas fitter, code-compliant repair, pressure-tested before sign-off.
