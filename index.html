Questions

What to know before you call.

If you don’t see it answered here, dial — Shane will pick up.

Do you offer free estimates? +

Yes — every job starts with a free, written estimate. No surprise pricing, no upsell scripts, no third-party dispatcher.

Are you licensed and insured? +

Yes. Fully licensed in Massachusetts and fully insured. License number provided on every written estimate and invoice.

How fast do you respond? +

Calls answered live during business hours, and returned within one hour after-hours. True emergencies (no heat in January, burst pipe, no hot water) — we come out, including nights and weekends.

What areas do you serve? +

Based in Saugus, MA. Working homes and jobsites across the Northshore and into Boston — Lynn, Revere, Malden, Melrose, Wakefield, Peabody, Stoneham, Everett, Somerville, Boston, Lexington, Tewksbury, Georgetown, Haverhill, and beyond. Outside the list? Call — if we’re close, we’ll help.

What payment methods do you take? +

Cash, check, all major credit cards, and Venmo. Payment is due on completion of the work.

Do you guarantee your work? +

Yes. One-year guarantee on all labor. Manufacturer warranty covers parts and equipment (typically 5–12 years on boilers and water heaters — we’ll spell out the exact warranty before install).

Do you handle new construction and additions? +

Yes — rough-in to finish. Plumbing for new builds, additions, and gut renovations. Inspected, signed off, ready to occupy.

Do you work weekends and holidays? +

24/7 for true emergencies. Same-day Saturday for boiler and water heater failures whenever possible. You call, Shane answers.
