Lynn has the densest stock of pre-war and triple-decker housing on the North Shore. That means cast iron drains, lead-to-galvanized water mains, and old boilers running well past their service life. We work Lynn weekly — most emergency calls on the North Shore come from this side of the line.

What we see in Lynn

Pre-war triple-deckers

Multi-family buildings throughout Lynn share waste stacks and old plumbing that hasn't been touched in decades. We've done full re-pipes and sectional repairs in dozens of these buildings.

Older boiler stock

1970s and 1980s cast iron boilers are still common in Lynn. Some are worth saving, many are not — we tell you straight.

Hard water means short water heater life

Lynn's water hardness shortens tank life. Tankless conversions and water-treatment options make sense for some homes — we'll quote both.
