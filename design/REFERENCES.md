# References — S. Meredith Plumbing & Heating

> A reference is evidence about ONE specific trait, never permission to copy a look. Each entry says what may and may not be borrowed. GitHub form-pattern sources are recorded separately in `_context/form-pattern-research-2026-10-10.md`.

## Shane's own van

- URL: `public/images/van/van-side.jpg` (owner photo)
- Trait studied: a plain list of the work, in the owner's own words and order, next to the phone number, readable from a driveway
- May borrow: the idea of presenting services as a short plain list of nouns; the order Shane chose. **Hero option 4 only** (Irving's brief of 2026-10-10 asks for a composition inspired by the van lettering): the bold italic bullet-list style, the logo decal beside it and the license line as printed
- Must not borrow: any wording that is not on the van; "Furnaces" until Shane confirms the service (S5); a drawing or photo-real rendering of the van itself; the van's layout anywhere outside option 4
- Evidence: `assets/refs/van-side-list.txt` (transcription of the lettering)

## GOV.UK Design System: question pages and error summary

- URL: https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/
- Trait studied: one question per page versus several on a page, and how errors are summarized and focused
- May borrow: the principle that short related questions may share a page; error summary at the top that links to each invalid field; keeping answers on error
- Must not borrow: the visual identity (GOV.UK colours, crown, Transport typeface) or its component CSS
- Evidence: `_context/form-pattern-research-2026-10-10.md`

## Shane's logo

- URL: `public/images/logo-full.png` (owner-supplied, re-exported from `_context/harvest/logo.jpg`)
- Trait studied: the charcoal/red palette, the crossed pipe wrenches, the house, the condensed wordmark, the phone number in the red base
- May borrow: its colours (already sampled into tokens), pipes and wrenches as drawn motifs, the logo itself placed whole and unchanged
- Must not borrow: redrawn, traced, cropped, recoloured or animated versions of the logo; new marks that imitate it; the wordmark lettering for other words
- Evidence: `design/ASSETS.json` (logo, logo-hero), `tests/e2e/heroes.review.mjs` (rendered at its own proportions)
